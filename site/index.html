Notes for leaders running AI at scale

Abstract

These notes are for executives who sponsor large AI and agentic programs and need decisions that hold up in production.

Each note starts from the engineering standard underneath the topic, explains what it means in plain terms, and ends with requirements a leader can put into a plan, a platform standard or a contract. Every requirement links back to its source.

The key words MUST, SHOULD and MAY in the requirements sections are to be read as described in RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 when, and only when, they appear in capitals.
