The route, stage by stage

Govern is charted last but holds from the first decision

  1. 1Frame

    Is this worth building, and what would prove it?

    • Rules of Machine LearningGoogle. Guide, stable.

    Also read here

    • AI RMF 1.0, charted at Govern. Framework, stable.

    Move on when there is a named owner, a decision this will change, and a measure of success agreed before building.

  2. 2Pilot

    Does it work on real data, measured honestly?

    • Holistic Evaluation of Language ModelsStanford CRFM · 2022. Paper, stable.

    Also read here

    • Rules of Machine Learning, charted at Frame. Guide, stable.

    Move on when results on real data beat a simple baseline, and the failures are written down, not only the average.

  3. 3Launch

    Can we ship it safely to real users?

    • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025OWASP Gen AI Security Project · 2024. Guide, evolving.

    Move on when a staged rollout, a tested rollback and a human escalation path are live before the first user.

  4. 4Scale

    Can it grow in users, agents and cost without losing control?

    • Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning SystemsNeurIPS · 2015. Paper, stable.

    Move on when the cost of a completed task is known, and the platform enforces limits on spend and agent loops.

  5. 5Operate

    Can we see, fix and improve it in production?

    GN-0001 · Draft Agentic observability , original note
    • Semantic conventions for generative AI systemsOpenTelemetry. Specification, evolving.

    Also read here

    • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, charted at Launch. Guide, evolving.
    • Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems, charted at Scale. Paper, stable.

    In control when every request leaves one connected trace, incidents are reviewed, and each lesson becomes an evaluation.

  6. 6Govern

    Who is accountable, and can we prove it?

    • AI RMF 1.0NIST · 2023. Framework, stable.

    Also read here

    • Semantic conventions for generative AI systems, charted at Operate. Specification, evolving.

    In control when every system has an accountable owner and a record of what it did, on whose authority and why.
