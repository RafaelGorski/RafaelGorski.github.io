1Frame

Is this worth building, and what would prove it?

Move on when there is a named owner, a decision this will change, and a measure of success agreed before building.

  1. Rules of Machine Learning: Best Practices for ML Engineering

    Google

    Guide · Stable · also Pilot

    Why it matters
    Its first rule is not to be afraid to launch without machine learning. A fair test for any AI proposal before money is committed: does the simple version already prove the need?
    What to check
    Is there a heuristic baseline, and is the success metric observable and attributable before any model is trained?

Also read here: AI RMF 1.0 (Govern)

2Pilot

Does it work on real data, measured honestly?

Move on when results on real data beat a simple baseline, and the failures are written down, not only the average.

  1. Holistic Evaluation of Language Models

    Stanford CRFM · 2022

    Paper · Stable

    Why it matters
    Shows how a single accuracy figure hides the trade-offs that matter, such as calibration, robustness and efficiency. Ask for that breadth before a pilot is called a success.
    What to check
    Are results reported per scenario and across several metrics, on an evaluation set fixed before testing began?

Also read here: Rules of Machine Learning (Frame)

3Launch

Can we ship it safely to real users?

Move on when a staged rollout, a tested rollback and a human escalation path are live before the first user.

  1. OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025

    OWASP Gen AI Security Project · 2024

    Guide · Evolving · also Operate

    Why it matters
    The shared list of ways LLM applications are attacked, from prompt injection to excessive agency. A launch review that cannot answer every item is not finished.
    What to check
    For each risk on the list, which control, test or monitor exists today, and who owns it?

4Scale

Can it grow in users, agents and cost without losing control?

Move on when the cost of a completed task is known, and the platform enforces limits on spend and agent loops.

  1. Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems

    NeurIPS · 2015

    Paper · Stable · also Operate

    Why it matters
    The model is a small part of a production ML system. Cost and fragility accumulate in the glue, data dependencies and feedback loops around it, which is where scaling budgets go.
    What to check
    Are data dependencies, configuration and feedback loops inventoried, owned and tested like code?

5Operate

Can we see, fix and improve it in production?

In control when every request leaves one connected trace, incidents are reviewed, and each lesson becomes an evaluation.

GN-0001 · Draft Agentic observability , original note

  1. Semantic conventions for generative AI systems

    OpenTelemetry

    Specification · Evolving · also Govern

    Why it matters
    A shared vocabulary for tracing agents, model calls and tool calls, so that cost, quality and incidents can be attributed across vendors and teams.
    What to check
    Do traces follow the gen_ai conventions end to end, with the convention version recorded per service?

Also read here: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 (Launch), Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems (Scale)

6Govern

Who is accountable, and can we prove it?

In control when every system has an accountable owner and a record of what it did, on whose authority and why.

  1. Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

    NIST · 2023

    Framework · Stable · also Frame

    Why it matters
    Organises AI risk into four functions, Govern, Map, Measure and Manage, with Govern cutting across the others. A common language for boards, auditors and engineering.
    What to check
    Does each production AI system have a documented owner, risk tolerance and measurement plan?

Also read here: Semantic conventions for generative AI systems (Operate)
