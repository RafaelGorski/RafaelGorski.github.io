About

These notes

Large AI programs fail less often on model quality than on the things around the model: how work is decomposed, how agents are observed, how cost and risk are governed, and how decisions made in a steering committee survive contact with production.

These notes are written for the people who make those decisions. Each one is grounded in a public engineering standard or specification, explained without jargon, and closed with requirements that can be adopted as written. The aim is guidance an executive can act on and an engineer can check.
